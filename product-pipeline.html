Product Pipeline — Gate Board

Updated 2026-07-29 · every candidate must clear ALL gates before any name / logo / site / script is built
THE GATE — one fail = killed, not adapted
  1. MOQ = exactly 1 retail unit (not "1 liter", not a 100-set tier)
  2. No bulk / per-litre / per-kg B2B raw goods
  3. No liquids, aerosols, gels or flammables (air-freight + hazmat + leak returns)
  4. No inventory buy-in — dropship or dead
  5. No medical or health-claim products (FTC)
  6. Not Amazon-commoditized (no cheaper multi-piece version under our price)
  7. Sell > $30, matching or slightly beating real competitor prices
  8. Profit ≥ 2.5–3× COGS → landed COGS + shipping ≤ ~$12
  9. Dropship-speed transit (30–35 day ocean = fail)
  10. Audience 30–50, Facebook-only
  11. Demo-able in a Reel — visible result in 3 seconds, no on-screen text
  12. Problem-solver with a nameable mechanism, not a novelty
  13. Own-label achievable at low/no MOQ
  14. 3-source compare before lock · exact Alibaba listing = media truth
