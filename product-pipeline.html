Updated 2026-07-29 · every candidate clears ALL 14 gates before any name / logo / site / script is built
THE GATE — one fail = killed, not adapted
  1. MOQ = exactly 1 retail unit
  2. No bulk / per-litre / per-kg B2B goods
  3. No liquids, aerosols, gels, flammables
  4. No inventory buy-in — dropship or dead
  5. No medical or health-claim products
  6. Not Amazon-commoditized
  7. Sell > $30 vs real competitor prices
  8. Profit ≥ 2.5–3× COGS
  9. Dropship-speed transit
  10. Audience 30–50, Facebook-only
  11. Demo-able in 3 seconds, no on-screen text
  12. Problem-solver with a nameable mechanism
  13. Own-label at low/no MOQ
  14. 3-source compare · exact Alibaba listing = media truth

WHY THIS LIST IS SHORT

Seven candidates were killed this round against two kept. The gate is deliberately narrow — gate 6 (not Amazon-commoditized) plus gate 7 (>$30) eliminates almost every cheap viral gadget, and gate 3 (no liquids) removes most of the home-care aisle. Padding the board with marginal picks would defeat the point of writing the gate. Next expansion pass should mine Ad Library verticals we have not touched: garage/workshop, outdoor/garden, and baby — all 30–50 Facebook-native.
